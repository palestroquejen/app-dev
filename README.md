# My Current Favorite Anime Series: bang dream ave mujica
 
## About
Ave Mujica is a Japanese all-female gothic and symphonic metal band created by Bushiroad as part of the BanG Dream! media franchise. Formed in 2023, the project spans real-world live concerts, voice acting, mobile games, and a dark television anime series.
 
### Why I like it
- the songs are good
- i like the story flow
- Heavy Symphonic Metal Sound: You might enjoy the dramatic contrast between fast guitar riffs, aggressive double-bass drumming, and haunting pipe organ or choir melodies.
- Theatrical Live Shows: Their real-world concerts play out like gothic stage plays. The voice actresses perform in masquerade masks and elaborate dresses while acting out spoken-word monologues between songs.
- The contrast between their glamorous masked stage personas and their messy real-life struggles keeps me hooked.
